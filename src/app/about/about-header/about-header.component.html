As a developer, I merge creativity with technology to turn ideas into interactive and functional realities. To me, art, design, and development are interconnected parts of my creative process, bridging the gap between vision and reality.

My curious and artistic nature led me to develop a deep passion for art, which started when I was just 10 years old and continued to grow throughout my high school days. I consistently participated in events and competitions related to art, where I could showcase my creativity and push my boundaries. These experiences not only refined my techniques but also fueled my competitive drive, motivating me to constantly improve and evolve as an artist. Every challenge I faced became an opportunity to learn and grow, allowing me to explore different styles and mediums. This dedication to art became a foundation for my creative journey, shaping the skills I now apply in design and development.

Through college, I developed my front-end development skills, and since then, I was hooked on designing websites. With my artistic vision, I was able to integrate my art skills into the development process, creating visually appealing and functional designs. This fusion of creativity and technology allowed me to approach each project with a unique perspective, focusing on both aesthetics and user experience. I found joy in transforming ideas into interactive digital platforms that not only look great but also provide seamless functionality. This passion continues to drive me as I explore new ways to innovate and grow in the ever-evolving world of web design.
